Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my Plan2Day Pro purchase?

Open the Plan2Day Pro screen in the app and tap Restore. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Plan2Day Pro. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.

Are my tasks synced or backed up?

With Plan2Day Pro you can turn on iCloud sync in Settings. Your tasks then sync through your own private iCloud to your iPhone and Mac. Without sync, tasks are stored only on your iPhone, and we can't recover them if the app is deleted. Either way, task text clears at midnight.

Why can't I pick some rhythms?

Eat the Frog, Energy Batching and Eisenhower are part of Plan2Day Pro. Rule of 3 and 1-3-5 also have a maximum number of tasks, so they're unavailable on days when you've captured more tasks than they can hold.

Voice capture isn't working.

Check that Plan2Day has microphone access in Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone. Speech is processed on your device.
