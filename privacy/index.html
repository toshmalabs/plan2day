Plan2Day

Privacy Policy

Effective 25 January 2027

In short: Plan2Day does not collect any data. There is no account, no analytics and no tracking. Your tasks stay on your devices, and in your own private iCloud only if you turn on iCloud sync.

This policy explains how the Plan2Day app ("Plan2Day", "the app"), made by Toshma Labs ("we", "us"), handles information.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. The app has no sign-in, contains no analytics or advertising software, and does not send your data to us or to any server we operate.

Information stored on your device

Your tasks, notes, reflections, settings and planning history are saved on your device. We cannot see or access them. Deleting the app deletes this data from that device.

iCloud sync (optional, Plan2Day Pro)

If you turn on iCloud sync in the app's Settings, your tasks, reflections and planning history are also stored in your own private iCloud database, using Apple's CloudKit, so they appear on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. This data is stored and encrypted by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. It is private to your Apple Account: we have no server, no access to your iCloud data and no way to read it. Task text is still deleted at midnight, on every device and in iCloud. Turning sync off stops it; data already on your devices stays there.

Exporting insights

Plan2Day Pro can export your Insights summary as a PDF or CSV file through the share sheet. The file contains daily counts only, never task text, and goes only where you choose to send it.

Microphone and speech recognition

If you choose to add tasks by voice, the app asks for permission to use the microphone. Your speech is converted to text on your device using Apple's on-device speech recognition and, where supported, Apple's on-device language model. Audio and transcripts are not sent to us or to any server, and audio is not saved.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules notifications locally on your device. No notification content leaves your device.

Purchases

Plan2Day Pro subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. Apple's handling of this information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Plan2Day does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above.
